# CursorRacing
 
Sharpen your reflexes and take control like never before in Cursor Racing! This top-down racing game puts the power in your hands. Glide through the track, finding the perfect balance between acceleration and grip as you chase the fastest lap time.

The clock is ticking, and only pinpoint precision will keep you from spinning out. Whether hugging the curves or flying down straightaways, every movement counts.

From flat-out fast corners to slow technical hairpins, each track demands finesse and focus. Do you have what it takes to dominate the track? Strap in, take control, and race to victory! 

Made with Unity Engine.
